## 3. Core Body Sections [Content Writer]

### Section 1 — The honest bit

**Heading:** You already know how this usually goes.

You leave the salon and the style is beautiful. Two days later your scalp is still
sore. Two months later the edges are thinner than they were, and you're back to
searching for another salon.

That is not what protective styling is supposed to do.

A protective style is meant to buy your hair a rest — six, eight weeks of not being
pulled, not being combed dry, not being fought with. If you come out of it with less
hair than you went in with, the style wasn't protecting anything.

So at Flourish Roots we work the other way round. The health of your hair is the job.
The style is what it looks like when the job is done well.

### Section 3 — How a visit goes

**Heading:** What happens when you come in
**Sub:** No surprises, no upselling, no five-hour mystery.

**1. We look before we touch.**
Scalp, edges, ends, density. If your hair isn't ready for the style you booked,
we'll say so and offer you something that works today.

**2. Detangle with patience.**
Damp hair, sectioned, worked from the ends up. This is the part most salons rush.
It's the part that decides how much hair you keep.

**3. Style at a tension your scalp can live with.**
If it stings, tell us. We adjust, and we mean it. A style shouldn't need painkillers.

**4. You leave knowing what to do next.**
How to wash it, how to sleep in it, when to take it down, and what to watch for.
Written down, not just said at the door.

### Section 9 — Quick answers

**Heading:** Before you book

**Do I need a deposit?**
Yes for the longer styles — 50% for mini twists and mini braids, 70% for micro twists.
It holds your slot. You'll see the amount before you confirm.

**What if I'm running late?**
You have 20 minutes' grace. After that we may need to reschedule you, or a late fee
applies, because someone is booked behind you.

**Can I bring my own extensions?**
Yes. Bring them clean and pre-stretched if you can.

**My hair is relaxed / transitioning. Can you still do it?**
Yes. Tell us at booking so we plan the right amount of time and the gentler approach.

**Do you do children's hair?**
Yes — threading and braids especially. Note it in your booking so we allow for it.
