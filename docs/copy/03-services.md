## 3. Core Body Sections [Content Writer]

### Intro — how to choose

**Heading:** Choosing a style, honestly

Three things decide what suits you: your hair's current condition, how long you want
the style to last, and how much time you can give a wash day.

- **Hair that's fragile right now** → threading, flat twists, or a treatment first.
- **You want six weeks off your hair** → mini twists, mini braids, micro twists.
- **You want length stretched without heat** → African threading / KiKo.
- **You want to grow your edges back** → nothing that pulls the hairline, plus a coaching session.

Tell us at booking what your hair has been through recently. It changes what we recommend.

### Section — Booking terms, said plainly

**Heading:** Before you book, three things

**1. Deposits hold your slot.**
50% for mini twists, mini braids, natural hair braids, Bantu knots and mini or micro
twist loosening. 70% for micro twists and sister locs. Transfer to the salon
account shown at checkout and send proof to the office line. Until the deposit lands,
the slot is still open to others.

**2. Time matters here.**
Please arrive at your appointment time. There's 20 minutes' grace. After that we may
have to reschedule you, or a late fee of ₦3,000 applies (₦5,000 on micro twists, sister locs and SAT with weaves),
because someone is booked behind you.

**3. Cancelling or rescheduling.**
Cancelling or moving an appointment after paying means 50% of what you paid is
forfeited, whether you paid in part or in full. We know that's firm. It's what lets
us hold a whole day open for one head.
