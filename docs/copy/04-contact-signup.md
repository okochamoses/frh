### Section 7 — Booking policy, in plain words

**Heading:** Our booking policy

**Deposits.** Mini twists and mini braids need 50% upfront. Micro twists need 70%.
Transfer to the salon account we send you and forward proof to the office line.
Your slot isn't confirmed until that lands.

**Arrival.** Come at your appointment time. You have 20 minutes' grace. After that
we may need to reschedule you, or a late fee applies — ₦3,000 on most services,
₦5,000 on micro twists — because another client is booked behind you.

**Cancelling or rescheduling.** After payment, cancelling or moving your appointment
means 50% of what you paid is forfeited, whether you paid in part or in full.
Rescheduling without prior notice is treated the same way.

**Micro twists** are weekdays only. Weekends are no longer workable for that service.

**Loosening for mini and micro twists** is weekend-only `[confirm]`. Fuller and longer
hair may attract more, at our discretion — and we'll tell you the number before we
start, never after.

**Extensions and beads.** You're welcome to bring your own extensions; clean and
pre-stretched, please. We don't sell beads. Attaching beads you bring is +₦500.

### Section 8 — Quick answers

**Heading:** Common questions

**Can I book for the same day?**
Sometimes, for the shorter styles. Use WhatsApp — it's faster than the form for today.

**Can I book for someone else?**
Yes. Put their name in the booking and your number in the contact field, and tell us
in the notes.

**Do you do children's hair?**
Yes, threading and braids especially. Mention it in the notes so we allow the time.

**My hair is relaxed. Can I still come?**
Yes. Tell us at booking so we plan a gentler approach and the right amount of time.

**What if I need to move my appointment?**
Message us as early as you can. If you've paid, the policy above applies — but early
notice always gives us more room to help.
