### Section 1 — The beginning

**Heading:** It started with the same complaint, over and over.

`[Mariam to confirm the true origin — the paragraphs below are the shape of the story,

Women would sit down and say a version of the same thing. *My edges are going.
My hair doesn't grow past a point. The last salon braided me so tight I couldn't sleep.
I've stopped bothering.*

The hair itself was rarely the problem. 4C hair is dense, tightly coiled, and it holds
its shape beautifully — but it is dry by nature and it does not forgive rough handling.
Combed dry, it snaps. Braided tight, it lets go at the hairline. Left in a style too
long, it locks and mats at the roots.

None of that is a flaw in the hair. It's a gap in how the hair is being handled.

So Mariam trained, practised, and opened Flourish Roots `[year]` to close that gap —
first `[at home / from a small space / …]`, then at Shop 303, Destiny Plaza on

### Section 3 — What we believe

**Heading:** How we work, and why

**1. The hair comes first, then the style.**
Every appointment begins with a look at your scalp, edges and ends. If the hair
isn't ready for what you booked, we say so.

**2. Gentle is a technique, not a mood.**
Damp detangling. Sectioning. Working from the ends up. Tension you can sleep in.
These are trainable, repeatable methods — which is why every stylist here learns them
the same way.

**3. Honesty over upselling.**
We will tell you when you don't need a treatment. We will tell you when a style you
saw online won't sit well on your density. We would rather keep you for years than
maximise one visit.

**4. You should leave able to look after it.**
Advice at the door isn't enough. You get a written routine — wash day, night care,
take-down timing — because what happens in the six weeks after matters more than
the six hours in our chair.

**5. Nobody gets shamed here.**
Relaxed, transitioning, matted, neglected while you were surviving something —
we've seen all of it. You'll get help, not a lecture.
