# Chidhama Imagery Direction

## Overall photography style

The visual language should feel:

- Warm
- Natural
- Editorial
- Authentic
- Premium
- Calm

The store itself provides a useful visual anchor: cream walls, warm timber slats, arched mirrors, black furniture accents, and warm lighting.

## Color behavior in photography

Photography should naturally contain:

- Warm wood
- Cream / ivory
- Deep neutral accents
- Soft gold lighting
- Natural greens

Avoid artificially color-grading every image to the exact UI palette.

## Salon interior

Use:

- Symmetry where possible
- Architectural details
- Arched mirrors
- Wood slat walls
- Warm pendant lighting
- Clean styling stations
- Plants as restrained accents

## Hair portraits

Use close, editorial compositions.

Prefer:

- Natural texture
- Curls
- Braids
- Locs
- Healthy hair
- Warm side lighting

## Product images

Use:

- Amber bottles
- Natural stone
- Wood
- Leaves
- Soft shadows

Product images should have enough negative space for UI copy where they are used as promotional imagery.
